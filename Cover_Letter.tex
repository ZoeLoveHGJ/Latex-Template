% Cover Letter Template

% 使用说明：
%   1. 填写论文信息 (\papertitle, \journalname)

%   2. 填写发件人 / 收件人信息
%   3. \makeheader 生成信头，\makeclosing 生成结尾
%   4. 主题色可选: \academictheme{red/purple/blue}
%
% 编译方式: xelatex Cover_Letter（一次即可）
% --------------------------------------------------------
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\documentclass{class/cover_letter}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 基本宏包配置
%----------------------------------------------------------
\usepackage[english]{babel}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 主题色配置 (可选: red, purple, blue)
%----------------------------------------------------------
\academictheme{red}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 论文信息（定义一次，全局复用）
% - Subject 行自动由 \papertitle + \journalname 生成
% - 正文中用 \thepapertitle 引用带引号的标题
% - 正文中用 \thejournalname 引用带斜体的期刊名
%----------------------------------------------------------
\papertitle{Your Paper Title Goes Here}
\journalname{IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 机构 Logo（可选，显示在首页页眉左侧）
%----------------------------------------------------------
% \institutionlogo{figure/university_logo.pdf}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 发件人信息 (Sender)
%----------------------------------------------------------
\sendername{Prof. Xiaolin Jia}
\senderaffiliation{Department of Computer Science \\ University Name}
\senderaddress{Xi'an, Shaanxi, China, 710000}
\senderemail{my\_jiaxl@163.com}
% \senderphone{+86-xxx-xxxx-xxxx}    % 可选

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 收件人信息 (Recipient)
%----------------------------------------------------------
\recipientname{Dr. Editor Name}
\recipienttitle{Editor-in-Chief}
\recipientaddress{Editorial Office}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 称呼与结束语
%----------------------------------------------------------
\opening{Dear Dr. Editor,}
\closing{Sincerely,}

%----------------------------------------------------------
% 正文
%----------------------------------------------------------
\begin{document}

\makeheader

%% ===== 正文部分 =====
%% 在此撰写投稿信内容
%% 可直接使用 \thepapertitle 和 \thejournalname 引用论文/期刊名

Please consider our manuscript entitled \thepapertitle\ for publication in \thejournalname.

This manuscript studies an important research problem and provides a clear technical contribution with practical relevance. The main strengths of the submission include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item A clearly defined problem setting and research objective
    \item A method, model, or system design that addresses the target problem
    \item Experimental, theoretical, or empirical evidence supporting the main claims
\end{itemize}

We believe this manuscript fits the scope of \thejournalname\ and will be of interest to its readership.

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to \thejournalname.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

\makeclosing

\end{document}
