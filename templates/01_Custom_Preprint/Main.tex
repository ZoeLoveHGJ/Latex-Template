\begin{abstract}
Tag identification in dense dynamic RFID systems poses significant throughput challenges under stochastic workload arrivals. This paper proposes a decoupled modular architecture that unifies modern authoring pipelines with major publisher formatting requirements (IEEE, Elsevier, ACM). By cleanly isolating scientific content from presentation layer mechanics, researchers can maintain a single source of truth while meeting diverse publication venues with zero migration overhead.
\end{abstract}
