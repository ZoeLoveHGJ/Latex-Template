\begin{abstract}
Tags identification in dense mobile RFID systems presents a major challenge due to the stochastic residence time of tags and severe communication collisions. This paper demonstrates a unified decoupled academic authoring framework that bridges modern modular writing with strict ACM SIG conference formatting standards.
\end{abstract}
